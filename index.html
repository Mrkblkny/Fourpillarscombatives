The mission

AVOID THE FIGHT.
BE READY IF YOU CAN'T.

Awareness. Contact Management. Respond. Get Home Safe.

The goal is to avoid fighting unless you have to—and get away as soon as safely possible. Recognize danger, manage contact, set boundaries, and leave when you can. If you must fight, be capable of defending yourself wherever the confrontation goes: on your feet, in the clinch, or on the ground. Train to protect yourself or others when necessary, stop the immediate threat, and disengage safely.

How we train ↗
Awareness first.Recognize problems early and avoid preventable violence.
Capable under pressure.Train striking, grappling, ground survival, and weapons awareness so you have options if you cannot leave.
Built for you.Weekly Monday and Wednesday group classes, private lessons, small groups, personal training, and events.
A firearm is not a substitute for fighting skills

CARRYING A FIREARM DOESN'T REPLACE FIGHTING SKILLS.

A confrontation can turn physical before you have room to react. Carrying a firearm does not replace striking, wrestling, grappling, or awareness. You need empty-hand skills when you cannot rely on a firearm—and sound judgment and control when physical contact happens while you are armed at close range.

01 / CONTACT DISTANCE

WHAT IF IT STARTS AT 0–5 FEET?

At arm's length, someone may grab you, crowd you, or force a clinch. Train for contact, movement, balance, and making safer decisions—not just ideal conditions at a distance.

02 / WEAPON AWARENESS

BEING ARMED IS NOT THE SAME AS BEING PREPARED.

When physical fighting and weapons risks overlap, you need more than marksmanship. We connect empty-hand skills with awareness, weapon retention concepts, responsible decisions, and safe disengagement.

03 / CITIZENS & PROFESSIONALS

TRAIN FOR YOUR LIFE. TRAIN FOR YOUR JOB.

Private citizens can train to protect themselves and others. Law enforcement, corrections, security, and military personnel can request instruction tailored to their responsibilities, equipment, policies, and the law.

The self-defense curriculum

FIVE SECTIONS.
ONE PURPOSE.

Our self-defense curriculum is organized into five sections designed to develop practical self-defense knowledge, fighting skills, physical fitness, and emergency preparedness.

01

ENVIRONMENTAL & SITUATIONAL AWARENESS

Learn to recognize potential threats, understand how criminals identify and select victims, manage unwanted contacts, avoid dangerous situations, and respond appropriately to developing threats.

02

EMPTY-HAND FIGHTING IN A WEAPONS ENVIRONMENT

Learn offensive and defensive fighting skills at different combat ranges, including striking, wrestling, clinch fighting, and ground fighting, while accounting for the possible presence of weapons.

03

WEAPONS FIGHTING & WEAPON DEFENSE

Learn which defensive weapons to consider carrying every day and how to use them safely and lawfully. Explore fighting with and defending against weapons, defensive shooting at longer distances and in close-contact situations including 0–5 feet and the clinch, weapon access, preventing an attacker from accessing weapons, weapon retention, and disarming.

04

FITNESS & ATTRIBUTES TRAINING

Develop the strength, power, endurance, mobility, speed, balance, coordination, and other physical attributes that support effective self-defense.

05

EMERGENCY MEDICAL & TRAUMA CARE

Learn essential first aid, bleeding control, emergency trauma care, CPR, medical equipment fundamentals, basic Tactical Combat Casualty Care (TCCC) concepts, and how to respond to injuries and emergencies. This section provides educational instruction only and does not award certifications. Additional medical lessons and handouts will be published as they are ready during public beta.

Our Philosophy: Recognize danger. Avoid unnecessary confrontation. Defend yourself when necessary. Provide emergency care when needed. Get Home Safe.

Common questions

TRAINING QUESTIONS.
CLEAR ANSWERS.

Learn what Four Pillars Combatives offers in Pearl, Mississippi and the Jackson Metro. For questions specific to your situation, use our training guide or contact the instructor.

When are the regular group classes?

Regular self-defense group classes meet Monday and Wednesday from 5:30 to 6:50 PM in the Pearl, Mississippi area. Confirm the current meeting location and availability before attending. See classes and pricing.

How much does group training cost?

Group self-defense memberships start at $75 per month. Check current membership terms and included classes on our Group Classes & Pricing page. Private sessions and workshops are separate.

Do you offer women's self-defense?

Yes. Women's self-defense is offered only through seminars, workshops, and private lessons by request. It is not a separate weekly class or small-group program. Women are also welcome in our regular self-defense classes, which are not women-specific. Explore women's self-defense.

Can I schedule private MMA or Eskrima lessons?

Yes. MMA, combat-sports and Eskrima instruction can be arranged by request through private lessons or suitable workshops. A single 60-minute one-person private lesson starts at $40. Explore private lessons.

Can my business, church or group host a seminar?

Yes. We offer custom local and traveling seminars for businesses, churches, community groups, martial arts schools and other organizations. Tell us your location, group size and training goals. Learn about seminars.

Is there an online training option?

Yes. Four Pillars Combatives Online Training is in PUBLIC BETA. Free accounts can access published free lessons plus Community Hub Events & Seminars, Private Messaging, and opt-in Find Training Partners. Founding and VIP options provide additional lessons and Community benefits. Explore Online Training for current availability.

Where do you train, and can I just walk in?

We are based in Pearl and serve the Jackson Metro. Sessions are held at arranged indoor or outdoor locations. Contact us to confirm your class location rather than expecting a walk-in storefront. Get in touch.

Can I book directly on the website?

No. The website sends a training inquiry; the instructor will confirm availability, session details and the location before anything is scheduled. Send an inquiry.

THE APPROACH

More than knowing how to fight.

Combat sports develop valuable fighting skills. Self-defense also requires knowing what to look for, when to leave, and what to do when a confrontation becomes physical.

01Recognize

Notice potential problems before you're forced to react.

02Manage

Use awareness, distance, movement, and clear boundaries.

03Respond & escape

Develop practical skills for getting away when avoidance fails.

THE SELF-DEFENSE CURRICULUM

What you'll learn.

Four connected sections, from awareness and contact management to physical skills and fitness.

SECTION 01

Situational Awareness

Recognize, avoid, and manage threats before a physical confrontation.

  • Environmental awareness
  • Contact Awareness & Management (CAM)
  • Boundaries, movement, and exits
SECTION 02

Empty-Hand Fighting

Use striking and grappling to survive a confrontation and create an opportunity to escape.

  • Striking and clinch
  • Ground survival and getting up
  • Weapons-Based Entanglement (WBE)
SECTION 03

Weapons Fighting

Account for weapons during close-range confrontations, including access, retention, and control during entanglements.

  • Armed-threat awareness
  • In-fight weapons access
  • Weapon retention and control
  • Weapons-Based Entanglement (WBE)
SECTION 04

Fitness & Attributes Training

Develop the physical qualities that support self-defense and fighting.

  • Strength, power, and endurance
  • Mobility and resilience
  • Timing, distance, and balance
Additional training: Eskrima and MMA lesson collections are included as material is added.

    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Before you join.

    What is Four Pillars Online Training?

    A private online training library built around the Four Pillars Combatives self-defense curriculum. Members can follow lessons and track their progress.

    What does my membership include?

    Access to the four-section core curriculum, the Eskrima and MMA collections, and future core lessons. Private lessons, seminars, and in-person training are separate.

    What does beta mean?

    Founding Members join during the early development of the platform. Courses and features will be added over time. Specific release dates are not guaranteed.

    Is there a monthly fee?

    No. Founding Membership is a one-time payment.

    What does lifetime access mean?

    Access to the core online library, including future core updates, for as long as Four Pillars Combatives operates the platform.

    When do I get access?

    After your one-time purchase is confirmed, you can sign in to your student dashboard. Your membership also covers new core lessons as they are released.

    Is this training beginner-friendly?

    Yes. Four Pillars Combatives Online Training is designed for beginners and experienced practitioners alike. Lessons start with fundamental concepts and progressively build toward more advanced skills. No prior martial arts, self-defense, or firearms experience is required.

    How do I get started?

    Create an account, complete the one-time purchase, and sign in to your student dashboard.

    Are purchases refundable?

    The Founding Membership is a one-time purchase that provides lifetime access to the core online training library, including future additions. All purchases are final and nonrefundable, except as required by law. If you have questions about the membership or what is included during beta access, please contact us before purchasing.

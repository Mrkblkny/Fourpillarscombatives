The mission

THE GOAL ISN'T
TO WIN A FIGHT.
IT'S TO GET HOME.

Awareness. Contact Management. Respond. Get Home Safe.

Recognize developing danger early, manage contact through positioning, boundaries, and communication, and respond appropriately if escape is not yet safe. The goal is to disengage and get home safely—not to win a fight.

The self-defense curriculum

FOUR SECTIONS.
ONE PURPOSE.

Weapons, striking, grappling, and fitness are our core training pillars. The specialized self-defense topics we teach are organized within four curriculum sections, separate from how you book lessons or events.

01

SITUATIONAL AWARENESS

Environmental awareness, contact management, taking the initiative when justified, and responding when an attacker acts first or you lose the initiative.

02

EMPTY-HAND FIGHTING

Striking, clinch fighting, ground survival, and managing multiple attackers with escape as the priority.

03

WEAPONS FIGHTING

Armed-threat awareness, in-fight access, weapon retention, control, and lawful defensive application.

04

FITNESS & ATTRIBUTES

Strength, power, conditioning, mobility, timing, distance, balance, and resilience.

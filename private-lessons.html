PRIVATE LESSONS.
YOUR GOALS.

Learn at your pace with instruction tailored to your experience, interests and training needs. Private sessions can focus on practical self-defense, Eskrima, striking, wrestling, grappling, MMA and combat sports.

Available by appointment: private self-defense, Eskrima, specialized weapons-based entanglement, and dedicated boxing, Muay Thai, MMA, wrestling and submission grappling. These are not part of a separate weekly combat-sports class schedule.

Focus your training

COACHING BUILT AROUND YOU.

Private lessons are available by inquiry and subject to scheduling and location arrangements.

SELF-DEFENSE

Practical Skills

Awareness, contact management, survival striking, clinch work, safe disengagement and weapons awareness.

COMBAT SPORTS

Striking & Grappling

Boxing, Muay Thai, MMA striking, wrestling, submission grappling and no-gi fundamentals. MMA is offered by private lesson or seminar, not regular group class.

ESKRIMA & WBE

Weapons-Based Training

Eskrima concepts, defensive weapons awareness, clinch or ground problems where weapons may be present, and responsible decisions.

Combat Sports / By appointment

BOXING. MUAY THAI. MMA. GRAPPLING.

Combat-sports coaching is available through requested private lessons and subject-focused seminars, NOT as a separately scheduled weekly group class. Group classes teach practical self-defense with striking and grappling, but are not MMA competition classes.

STRIKING

Boxing & Muay Thai

Individualized coaching in stance, movement, pad work and controlled skill development appropriate to your experience and goals.

MMA

Mixed Martial Arts

MMA striking and integrated standing-to-grappling concepts taught in a private or custom seminar format, based on the topic requested.

GRAPPLING

Wrestling & No-Gi

Wrestling, submission wrestling, No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and practical grappling concepts in focused lessons.

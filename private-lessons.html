Focus your training

COACHING BUILT AROUND YOU.

Private lessons are tailored to your goal: self-defense as a private citizen, job-related defensive skills, dedicated Eskrima training, or boxing, Muay Thai, MMA and grappling coaching to develop as an athlete or prepare for competition. Training is by appointment and subject to location and instructor availability.

SELF-DEFENSE

Practical Skills

Recognizing danger, managing contact, practical striking, clinch and ground survival, weapons awareness, protecting yourself or others when necessary, and safely disengaging.

COMBAT SPORTS

Striking & Grappling

Boxing, Muay Thai, MMA striking, wrestling, submission grappling and no-gi fundamentals—with training tailored to your technical development or competition goals. Dedicated coaching is by private lesson or seminar, not a standing weekly combat-sports class.

ESKRIMA & CLOSE-RANGE SKILLS

Eskrima & Weapons-Based Self-Defense

Learn Eskrima fundamentals, movement, timing and weapons awareness. We also offer Weapons-Based Entanglement (WBE): training for close-range physical confrontations where someone may possess, access or need to retain control of a weapon.

Combat Sports / By appointment

BOXING. MUAY THAI. MMA. GRAPPLING.

Want to compete in MMA, boxing, Muay Thai, wrestling or submission grappling? We offer private coaching and focused seminars in sport-specific skills, controlled resistance and competition preparation. Our regular weekly group classes focus on self-defense, not dedicated combat-sports training.

Training for a match and preparing for a real-world confrontation have different goals. Combat sports build tested fighting skills against resistance. Self-defense adds the decision to avoid a fight whenever possible—and the ability to adapt and defend yourself if escape is blocked, then disengage when safe. Private coaching / close-range self-defense

YOU MAY CARRY A GUN. YOU STILL NEED FIGHTING SKILLS.

At 0–5 feet, an encounter may already involve a grab, a struggle for balance, or a clinch. We offer personalized instruction connecting practical striking and grappling with awareness of weapons-related risks. The goal is to protect yourself or others when necessary, make responsible decisions, and disengage safely.

Suitable for responsible civilian students and personnel in military, law enforcement, corrections, or security roles seeking supplementary individual instruction. Training is adapted to experience and applicable policies; firearms-related topics require appropriate safety arrangements and instructor approval.

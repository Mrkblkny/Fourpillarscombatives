What we can teach

LEARN OUR CURRICULUM. TRAIN FOR REAL SITUATIONS.

Choose a course based on what you want to learn: our four-section self-defense curriculum, a combat-sports discipline, Eskrima, women's self-defense, or a specialized combatives topic. We tailor the lesson scope and intensity to participants' experience, the length of the event, and the facility—not every seminar needs to cover everything.

01 / SELF-DEFENSE

Four Pillars Combatives Curriculum

Train the four sections: Situational Awareness, Empty-Hand Fighting, Weapons Fighting, and Fitness & Attributes. Work toward recognizing danger early, managing contact, developing the capability to defend yourself at different ranges and positions, protecting others when necessary, and getting home safely.

02 / COMBAT SPORTS

MMA, Boxing, Muay Thai & Grappling

Learn or sharpen boxing, Muay Thai, MMA, wrestling, submission wrestling, No-Gi grappling, and integrated striking or grappling skills. Available for sport-focused groups, martial arts schools, and hosts interested in how these foundations support self-defense.

03 / ESKRIMA

Eskrima & Weapons Awareness

Host an Eskrima workshop covering movement, coordination, distance, timing, and training methods for stick-based and empty-hand concepts. We can adapt instruction for beginners or martial artists looking to broaden their skills.

04 / WOMEN'S SAFETY

Women's Self-Defense

Practical awareness, boundaries, verbal skills, physical escapes, and getting to safety. An option for schools, businesses, churches, community groups, or a group of friends organizing their own workshop.

05 / SPECIALIZED

Close-Range Self-Defense & WBE

Focus on how a confrontation can change at 0–5 feet: unwanted contact, standing-to-clinch transitions, ground survival, and weapon-related risks. Carrying a firearm does not replace the ability to move, grapple, and make sound decisions at close range. Topics are matched to participants, equipment, and safety needs.

06 / GROUP EVENTS

Schools, Workplaces & Private Groups

Bring tailored self-defense, personal safety, or martial-arts instruction to a school, business, gym, church, security team, or private group. Content is adapted to age, responsibilities, policies, and applicable law.

Professional and unit training by request

MILITARY, LAW ENFORCEMENT & SECURITY. TRAIN FOR CONTACT.

Four Pillars Combatives welcomes inquiries from military personnel, military groups, law enforcement, corrections, private security, and other professionals who may face physical confrontation in the course of their duties. We can discuss a course for your team locally or at a suitable host facility elsewhere.

Potential subjects include awareness and contact management, empty-hand fighting, clinch and ground survival, weapon-related risks, Weapons-Based Entanglement, and practical conditioning. Specialized instruction is scoped to the group's objectives, skill level, equipment, safety protocols, applicable law, and organizational policies. All training is arranged by request; no military endorsement or existing agency contract is implied.

MILITARY

PUBLIC SAFETY

Law Enforcement & Corrections

SECURITY & INDUSTRY

Course formats

FROM A SHORT INTRODUCTION TO MULTI-DAY TRAINING.

We offer different formats depending on how deeply your group wants to train. A short workshop can introduce important concepts; longer courses allow more guided practice and a broader curriculum focus.

Each event is quoted individually. Group size, topics, date, facility, equipment, safety considerations, instructor availability, and travel are confirmed before booking.

INTRODUCTION

4-hour workshop

Introduce essential self-defense concepts or concentrate on one practical topic.

DEEPER PRACTICE

8-hour seminar

Spend more time learning, practicing, and applying selected skills with instructor guidance.

EXTENDED COURSE

2-day / 16-hour event

Explore a broader selection of curriculum material with additional practice and training scenarios, as appropriate.

Skills are perishable. A seminar is a valuable start or a way to deepen existing training—not a substitute for continued practice. Travel, venue, and other necessary expenses may apply.

REAL-WORLD SELF-DEFENSE. WHERE YOU ARE.

Four Pillars Combatives connects Situational Awareness, Empty-Hand Fighting, Weapons Fighting, and Fitness & Attributes Training. Avoid fighting when possible. If force becomes necessary, use your preparation to defend yourself or others, stop the immediate threat, and disengage safely. Combat-sports skills and Eskrima help inform this integrated approach.

A focused seminar may cover one part of the curriculum; a longer course can explore several. Skills are perishable, so follow-up practice matters. No brief event can make someone fully prepared or teach every subject.

What we can teach

LEARN OUR CURRICULUM. TRAIN FOR REAL SITUATIONS.

Choose a course based on your goal: personal self-defense as a private citizen, job-related defensive skills, combat-sports competition, Eskrima, women's self-defense, or specialized combatives. We tailor the lesson scope and intensity to participants' experience, the length of the event, and the facility—not every seminar needs to cover everything.

01 / SELF-DEFENSE

Four Pillars Combatives Curriculum

Train the four sections: Situational Awareness, Empty-Hand Fighting, Weapons Fighting, and Fitness & Attributes. Work toward recognizing danger early, managing contact, developing the capability to defend yourself at different ranges and positions, protecting others when necessary, and getting home safely.

02 / COMBAT SPORTS

Combat Sports

Host training in MMA, boxing, Muay Thai, wrestling, and submission grappling, including No-Gi grappling. We can tailor sessions to athletic development, competition preparation, or a martial arts school’s specific training goals. Combat sports can be taught for their own sake—not only as self-defense foundations.

03 / ESKRIMA

Eskrima & Weapons Awareness

Host an Eskrima workshop covering movement, coordination, distance, timing, and training methods for stick-based and empty-hand concepts. We can adapt instruction for beginners or martial artists looking to broaden their skills.

04 / WOMEN'S SAFETY

Women's Self-Defense

Practical awareness, boundaries, verbal skills, physical escapes, and getting to safety. An option for schools, businesses, churches, community groups, or a group of friends organizing their own workshop.

05 / SPECIALIZED

Close-Range Self-Defense & WBE

Focus on how a confrontation can change at 0–5 feet: unwanted contact, standing-to-clinch transitions, ground survival, and weapon-related risks. Carrying a firearm does not replace the ability to move, grapple, and make sound decisions at close range. Topics are matched to participants, equipment, and safety needs.

06 / GROUP EVENTS

Schools, Workplaces & Private Groups

Bring tailored self-defense, personal safety, or martial-arts instruction to a school, business, gym, church, security team, or private group. Content is adapted to age, responsibilities, policies, and applicable law.

Private citizens and professional groups

SELF-DEFENSE FOR CITIZENS. TRAINING FOR PROFESSIONALS.

Our seminars are not limited to law enforcement or military personnel. Private citizens, families, martial arts schools, churches, and community groups can host practical self-defense instruction too. We also offer focused training for law enforcement, corrections, security, and other professionals whose jobs may require them to manage aggressive or physically resistant people.

The goals are different. Citizens generally prioritize recognizing danger, avoiding conflict, protecting themselves or others when necessary, and getting away safely. Law enforcement and other professionals may have duties to remain engaged, protect others, or control a situation—always within applicable law, policy, and their authorized role. Military personnel or groups may also inquire about training by request.

PRIVATE CITIZENS

Everyday Self-Defense

Bring our curriculum to your town, school, church, gym, neighborhood group, or private event. Learn awareness, boundaries, practical striking and grappling, weapons-related risks, and safer decisions in a confrontation.

LAW ENFORCEMENT & OTHER PROFESSIONALS

Job-Related Defensive Skills

Explore awareness, contact management, positional-control concepts, weapon-related risks, and practical fighting skills for law enforcement, corrections, security, and high-contact occupations. Content is adapted to duties, policies, and the law.

MILITARY / BY REQUEST

Military Personnel & Groups

Military personnel and approved groups may inquire about self-defense or close-range combatives instruction tailored to their objectives and training environment. Dates and course details are arranged with the requesting group.

TRAINING BUILT AROUND YOUR NEEDS.

From a one-hour introduction to a three-day course—or longer—we tailor seminars and workshops to your group's goals, experience, and available time.

Whether you want our complete self-defense curriculum, Eskrima, combat sports, or specialized combatives, we'll help design an event that fits.

Available locally and out of town for private citizens, martial arts schools, businesses, law enforcement, military personnel, and other groups.

REAL-WORLD SELF-DEFENSE. WHERE YOU ARE.

Four Pillars Combatives connects Situational Awareness, Empty-Hand Fighting, Weapons Fighting, and Fitness & Attributes Training. Avoid fighting when possible. If force becomes necessary, use your preparation to defend yourself or others, stop the immediate threat, and disengage safely. Combat-sports skills and Eskrima help inform this integrated approach.

A focused seminar may cover one part of the curriculum; a longer course can explore several. Skills are perishable, so follow-up practice matters. No brief event can make someone fully prepared or teach every subject.

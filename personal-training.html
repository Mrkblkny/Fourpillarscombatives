PERSONAL TRAINING.
BUILT FOR YOU.

You don't have to practice martial arts to work with us. Get individualized coaching in strength, power, cardiovascular conditioning, endurance, mobility and movement. For martial artists and other active clients, training can also develop useful physical attributes such as speed, agility, balance and coordination. Every program is adapted to your abilities and goals.

Available by appointment only: individual personal training sessions are scheduled separately and are not included in group-class memberships. Personal training focuses on fitness and physical attributes, not MMA, wrestling or other fighting-skill instruction; request a private martial arts lesson for those subjects.

Personalized fitness

MAKE PROGRESS WITH PURPOSE.

Work with a certified personal trainer on general fitness, conditioning, or the physical attributes that support martial arts and self-defense. You do not need martial arts experience to participate.

Fitness & Attributes Training: develop strength, power, endurance, mobility, resilience, speed, agility, balance and coordination. Timing and distance management are primarily practiced as martial skills in private lessons or group self-defense training, rather than promised as results of a general fitness workout.

01

Strength & Power

Develop practical strength and power through exercises selected for your experience, capabilities and goals.

02

Cardio & Endurance

Build aerobic fitness, repeat-effort capacity and stamina with progressive conditioning.

03

Mobility & Resilience

Train movement quality, flexibility, stability and physical resilience with appropriate exercises.

04

Combative Attributes

For clients who want it, develop speed, agility, balance, coordination, and movement control to support martial arts and self-defense.

What are combative attributes? They are physical qualities—such as speed, balance, agility, coordination, timing and distance management—that help you move and react effectively. Timing and distance are also practiced through appropriate skill drills; fitness sessions focus on building the underlying physical capacity. General fitness clients can train strength, cardio and mobility without participating in martial arts.

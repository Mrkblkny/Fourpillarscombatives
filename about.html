Mark began training in martial arts in 1996. His background includes boxing, Muay Thai, MMA, submission wrestling, no-gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Eskrima, and weapons-based combatives.

A former MMA fighter, Mark has lived and trained in an MMA gym and participated in immersive uchi-deshi training. His experience also includes U.S. Army and U.S. Navy service, corrections, private security, and personal training.

Those experiences helped shape an approach that values sound decisions, composure, preparation, physical capability, and avoiding unnecessary confrontation.

The philosophy

NO FALSE
CONFIDENCE.

Combat sports help develop timing, distance, conditioning, and the ability to perform against resistance. Self-defense adds the decisions that come before and during a real confrontation.

WHAT COMBAT SPORTS TEACH

Striking, wrestling, grappling, pressure, movement, controlled resistance, and the ability to work through difficult physical problems.

WHAT SELF-DEFENSE ADDS

Recognizing warning signs, managing contact, verbal boundaries, awareness of weapons, legal considerations, and making safe opportunities to leave.

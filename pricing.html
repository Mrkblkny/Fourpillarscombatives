PRIVATE & SMALL-GROUP LESSONS.

Private, semi-private, small-group, personal training, and live video sessions are 60 minutes and are booked separately from group memberships. For semi-private and small-group lessons, prices are for the entire session—not per person. Contact us to confirm availability.

Specialty availability: request boxing, Muay Thai, MMA, wrestling, submission wrestling, No-Gi grappling, Eskrima or focused self-defense through private instruction. Custom group seminars may also be available. All require a separate inquiry and are not included in group memberships. See combat-sports coaching options.

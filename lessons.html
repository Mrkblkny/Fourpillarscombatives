TRAVEL FEES.

There is no mileage or driving-time charge for training within Pearl, Mississippi. For training outside Pearl, the following round-trip travel charges apply in addition to the training fee.

Mileage$0.85 / mile

Round-trip mileage from Pearl to the training location and back.

Driving time$30 / hour

Total round-trip driving time, rounded up to the next 15-minute increment.

Other expensesAs needed

Parking, tolls, necessary lodging, meals when travel and training exceed 4 hours, and other applicable expenses.

Training fees are separate from travel expenses. Payment processing fees for noncash payments and applicable taxes may be additional.

What we focus on

PREVENT. RESPOND. ESCAPE.

Self-defense isn’t only about fighting. We prioritize avoiding danger and safely disengaging whenever possible.

01 / BEFORE CONTACT

Awareness & Prevention

Recognize warning signs, identify exits and plan safer choices when possible.

02 / MANAGE CONTACT

Boundaries & Movement

Use your voice, body position, space and movement to manage unwanted approaches.

03 / IF NECESSARY

Physical Survival & Escape

Practice practical striking, escapes, ground survival and awareness of possible weapons, always with an escape-first objective.

No previous training required

START WHERE YOU ARE.

Training can include recognition, verbal boundaries, safe positioning, escaping grabs, staying upright when possible, ground survival and making decisions under pressure.

We adapt drills to the participant and training environment. We do not promise that any technique guarantees safety.

Common questions

Do I need martial arts experience?

No. Beginner-friendly sessions can be adapted to your needs.

Do you have women-only weekly classes?

Women can participate in our regular group self-defense classes. For a women-focused private group session or workshop, contact us to arrange a custom event; a separate weekly women-only class is not currently advertised.

Can our workplace or church host a workshop?

Yes. Tell us your group size, location, goals and preferred date so we can discuss a custom event.

Where is training held?

Training is based around Pearl and the Jackson Metro. Locations vary by session and are confirmed in advance.

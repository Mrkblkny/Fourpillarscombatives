WOMEN'S SELF-DEFENSE.
GET HOME SAFE.

Real-world self-defense starts before a fight. Learn to recognize danger, establish boundaries, and avoid violence when possible. If you are grabbed, cornered, or attacked and cannot leave safely, train practical skills for protecting yourself and creating an opportunity to escape. Instruction is adapted to your experience and goals.

How to book: Women’s Self-Defense is offered only through seminars, workshops, and private lessons, by request. There is no separate weekly women’s self-defense class or independent small-group program. Women are welcome in our regular group self-defense classes, which are a different offering.

What we focus on

PREVENT. RESPOND. ESCAPE.

We prioritize avoiding danger and getting away safely. But when an attack cannot be avoided, fighting skills matter: defend yourself as necessary, stop the immediate threat, and leave when safe.

01 / BEFORE CONTACT

Awareness & Prevention

Recognize warning signs, identify exits and plan safer choices when possible.

02 / MANAGE CONTACT

Boundaries & Movement

Use your voice, body position, space and movement to manage unwanted approaches.

03 / IF NECESSARY

Physical Survival & Escape

Practice practical striking, escapes, ground survival, and awareness of possible weapons so you have more options when leaving immediately is not possible.

No previous training required

START WHERE YOU ARE.

Training can include recognition, verbal boundaries, safe positioning, escaping grabs, staying upright when possible, ground survival and making decisions under pressure.

We adapt drills to the participant and training environment. We do not promise that any technique guarantees safety.

Common questions

Do I need martial arts experience?

No. Beginner-friendly sessions can be adapted to your needs.

Do you have women-only weekly classes?

No. Women's Self-Defense is offered only through seminars, workshops, and private lessons by request. Women can still join regular group self-defense classes, but these are not women-only classes.

Can our workplace or church host a workshop?

Yes. Tell us your group size, location, goals and preferred date so we can discuss a custom event.

Where is training held?

Training is based around Pearl and the Jackson Metro. Locations vary by session and are confirmed in advance.

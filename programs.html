Training availability

WHAT IS AVAILABLE BY REQUEST?

The weekly group class is our regularly scheduled program. The following options are arranged separately; do not assume a specialty appears on the weekly class calendar.

Carrying a firearm does not replace the need for fighting skills. Ask about practical striking, clinch and grappling skills, and weapons awareness for close-distance confrontations, including situations that begin within 0–5 feet.

PRIVATE LESSONS

Combat Sports & Specialized Skills

MMA, boxing, Muay Thai, wrestling and submission grappling—including competition preparation—are available by private lesson. Also ask about Eskrima and Weapons-Based Entanglement (WBE), which addresses close-range physical struggles when weapons may be present.

PERSONAL TRAINING

Fitness & Attributes

One-on-one fitness: strength, power, cardiovascular conditioning, speed, agility, mobility, balance and coordination. No martial arts training required; personal training is not part of the class membership.

SEMINARS & WORKSHOPS

Custom Group Events

Locally hosted and traveling seminars for self-defense, Eskrima, combat sports (including MMA), women’s self-defense, close-range weapons-based topics, military personnel and groups, law enforcement, corrections, security teams, schools, workplaces, martial arts schools, and private groups. All arranged by request.

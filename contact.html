Quick answers

GOOD TO KNOW.

Do I need martial arts experience?

No. Beginners and experienced students are both welcome. Instruction can be adapted to your current level.

Do you offer women’s self-defense training?

Yes. Women’s self-defense is available through private instruction, custom small groups and workshops. Women are also welcome in our regular group classes.

Where does training take place?

Training locations vary depending on the class or type of instruction. We train indoors, in backyards, at parks, and in other suitable outdoor training environments. Private lessons, seminars, and workshops may also be held at an agreed-upon location. Please contact us before attending to confirm the current training location.

Are private lessons available?

Yes. Private lessons cover self-defense, Eskrima, and combat sports such as MMA, boxing, Muay Thai, wrestling and grappling. Two-person and small-group lessons are also available by appointment.

Do you offer seminars?

Yes. You can organize a local or traveling seminar in self-defense, Eskrima, combat sports, women’s self-defense or specialized combatives. Individuals, schools, gyms, businesses and other groups can inquire. Events can run from one hour to three days or longer.

Is there a free introductory lesson?

Yes, for local residents only. Use the free intro lesson link to request a time and confirm availability.

Can I book directly through the website?

No. The form sends an inquiry. We will confirm available times, event details, pricing where applicable, and the training location before anything is scheduled.
